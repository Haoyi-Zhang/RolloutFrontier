# Literature positioning and TPDS calibration

## Novelty boundary

The broad preservation mechanism predates this work. Warranties prevent writes from
invalidating active assertions; Homeostasis derives locally enforceable treaties;
Predictive Treaties makes the local-predicate implication explicit; and invariant
confluence characterizes wider coordination requirements. The paper therefore does
not claim invention of predicate reservation, local-to-global implication, or a
general coordination-avoidance theorem.

The combinatorial object also predates this work. Binary frontier members are maximal
bicliques and formal concepts; higher-arity closed product patterns have dedicated
enumeration literature; maximum edge biclique is NP-complete. The manuscript uses the
term availability frontier for the rollout interpretation, not for a new maximal-box
theory. The narrower contribution is the API-contract formulation, independently
reconstructible bounded selection, and durable acquisition/mutation/retirement
protocol with retained failure evidence.

## Reference integrity

The manuscript bibliography contains 78 scholarly sources and 88 citation occurrences.
All 78 sources are cited. `reference_audit.csv` records first-citation number, key,
exact title, year, category, verification basis, argumentative role, and citation
count. `reference_sources.csv` adds a publisher, DOI, official/institutional record,
or exact-title bibliographic lookup for every ordered entry.

This establishes a traceable bibliography and bounded claim use; it is not a claim
that all 78 papers were freshly reread in full. Claim-level checks were concentrated
on the closest predicate-preservation ancestors, the maximal-product novelty boundary,
MoonlightBox's assembly-time result, and the TPDS papers used for direct journal
positioning. Ordinary rule pages are workflow sources, not manuscript references.

## TPDS structural sample

`tpds_calibration.csv` replaces every previous target-calibration record. It contains
12 real TPDS articles spanning consistency, object sharing at the edge, survivable
updates, consensus and fault tolerance, epidemic consistency, decentralized
communication, staleness control, edge resource optimization, and geo-distributed
deployment. The table is metadata/abstract-level structural calibration. It does not
claim full-text rereading, reproduce those systems, or estimate acceptance
probability.

The recurring TPDS pattern relevant here is: state an explicit distributed model and
failure assumptions; identify one precise systems mechanism; separate theorem or
invariant from implementation evidence; report scale, negative cases, and resource
costs; and delimit the workload/fault population. The journal manuscript follows that
pattern by placing the exact finite objective, persistent lifecycle, negative controls,
search refusals, generated-holdout boundary, and same-host limitation in the main
paper.

## Direct neighboring lines

### Distributed consistency and authorization

Acute Cloud Types and optimistic causal consistency show how systems combine or
control consistency semantics. Bayou, Dynamo, COPS, CRDTs, and delta-state CRDTs
address weak connectivity, high availability, causal visibility, or convergence.
Those properties do not by themselves authorize future support mutations against a
cross-owner predicate. The live-hold protocol addresses that narrower lifetime issue.

Quiver is a direct TPDS edge-system comparator for consistent mutable object sharing;
survivable software-update work is a direct comparator for resilient update
distribution. Neither solves workload-specific correlated support authorization.

### Safe change and verification

Network-update and network-verification systems provide richer packet semantics,
transition mechanisms, and verification representations. They are used to clarify
what this positive-contract abstraction omits. Distributed testing and verified
systems work motivate separate oracles, negative controls, replay, model checking,
and process-level fault cuts; this artifact does not claim implementation refinement
proofs comparable to Verdi or IronFleet.

### API evolution and source lineage

API-evolution work studies mining, compatibility detection, migration, deprecation,
and Android fragmentation. The controller assumes its contract atoms are already
valid and performs none of those upstream tasks. MoonlightBox computes a lower bound
on one application's assembly time from the latest introduction among referenced APIs
and does not provide an upper bound or a distributed authorization mechanism. The
public slice in this project is used only to exercise declaration-history boundaries.

## Supported and unsupported claims

Supported: exact bounded frontier enumeration in the declared guard language;
independent finite-oracle agreement; a persistent single-writer hold lifecycle;
minimum frozen-vector obstructions; finite protocol checks; generated schedule
comparisons with retained unfavorable cases; and bounded same-host component/process
evidence.

Unsupported: a new maximal-product object, behavioral API equivalence, representative
fleet generalization, production deployment, WAN performance, Byzantine or permanent-
disk-loss tolerance, concurrent same-identity failover, unbounded exact synthesis,
probabilistic availability, or universal superiority over treaty/warranty frameworks.

## Labelled / grouped MSS primary comparison

Belov, Morgado, and Marques-Silva, *SAT-based Preprocessing for MaxSAT (extended
version)*, [author version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.2298v2): all 18 pages read.
Section 3.2 defines label-induced clause selection, hard unlabelled clauses and
label-level MSS/MCS; Section 4.1 gives selector-based weighted MaxSAT encoding.
Labels can activate a multi-clause semantic group. P011's acceptance frontier is
ordinary labelled-MSS machinery after hard constraints encode its finite guard
choices and strong all-corner safety. The bridge proof is in `proofs.md` and the
journal supplement, with a literal assignment/subset control in
`tests/labelled_mss_bridge.py`. This removes any interpretation that nondominated
profile-label sets constitute a new maximal-subset object.

Bendik et al., [*Tunable Online MUS/MSS Enumeration*](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2016.50),
all 13 pages read: Definition 1 distinguishes inclusion maximality from maximum
cardinality; Section 3 searches a monotone subset space. P011 does not evaluate a
new MUS/MSS enumeration engine against that algorithm. Product mass differs from
label cardinality, but can be supplied as an application objective on a standard
frontier; it is not by itself an algorithmic novelty claim. Canonical positive
guard representation and runtime durable obligations remain explicit additional
requirements, not evidence that general SAT machinery cannot express them.

The directly inspected sources do not implement P011's particular per-owner
durable hold lifecycle. That is a scoped observation, not a priority assertion:
warranty/treaty literature remains material and this revision has not established
an exhaustive novelty boundary.
