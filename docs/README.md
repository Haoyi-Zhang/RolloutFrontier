The article and supplement present the research argument. These design notes
supply the exact inputs, algorithms, measurement conventions and executable
entry points needed to inspect and reproduce its evidence.

The scale and execution guides describe different units: the planning study
uses catalog models through 1,024 owners, while the largest physical dataflows
run 32 separate owner processes on one host. The retained earlier cohorts keep
their own task inventories and implementation scope.
